**Justification.** The size is the OLB frame and Designer boilerplate. The screen itself is one single-record block on
`AGNTS` with medium logic: (1) a new authorization takes the user's port, the next `ATHRZTN_SEQ_NBR` of the port
(`MAX + 1`), status 1 (ساري) and the current Hijri date, and is logged with `ACTIVITY.ADD_ACTIVITY(9, 1, 1, …)`;
(2) importer and broker company must exist in the port, must not be suspended (`IMPR.IMPR_SUSPENDED`) and must differ;
(3) typing an existing `ATHRZTN_SEQ_NBR` queries that authorization; (4) a queried authorization can be changed only
when it is valid (status 1) and not yet used in an importer declaration (`DCLTN.IMPR_IMPR_AUTH_USED`), otherwise the
block is insert-only and UPDATE_STATUS tells why (messages 8-30 / 8-80); (5) the functions `CD1_STOPIMPRAUTH` and
`CD2_REUSEIMPRAUTH` cancel (status 2 + cancel date) / restore (status 1, no date) the authorization after a
confirmation, log activity 3 / 4 and COMMIT. Delete is not possible (KEY-DELREC is the NIB frame, every queried record
is made non-deletable).
